## What changed: six primary preprints

### 1. Directed null arrival times can reconstruct a continuum Lorentzian metric, conditional on calibrated clocks

**Maxim V. Churilov**, *Optical and ADM Tomography from Directed Causal Arrival Times*, **submitted 3 Nov 2026**; appeared on gr-qc's 13 Nov *new* feed.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14554
- Primary full public HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14554v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.14554 (DOI registration described as pending by arXiv at review).

For ADM geometry ds² = -N²dt² + h_ij(dx^i+beta^i dt)(dx^j+beta^j dt), impose Δ=N²-h(beta,beta)>0. Future null coordinate arrival time along a spatial direction v obeys

    F(v) = [h(beta,v) + sqrt(h(beta,v)^2 + Delta*h(v,v))]/Delta.

Its odd part omega(v)=[F(v)-F(-v)]/2 and squared even part a(v,v)=[(F(v)+F(-v))/2]^2 give r=a-omega⊗omega=h/Delta, beta=r^{-1}omega. The null cone recovers the metric *up to conformal scale*. Normalized spacetime volume then fixes local scale up to one global constant. With a supplied coordinate frame and operational clock foliation, the author derives d(d+3)/2 directional readings, **nine in d=3**, and an error law O(a/epsilon+epsilon+zeta). This is not a construction of clocks or causal arrival data from a background-free quantum theory.

**GTOR test:** starting with a proposed relational event/clock model, predict directed one-way arrival distributions, then attempt this inverse. Failure of the specific nine-direction inversion beyond its stated noise/conditioning assumptions rejects the continuum-arrival hypothesis for that configuration. A coordinate-dependent shift is not, alone, an emergent physical field.

### 2. Strong, operational pure-state measurements exist within ordinary projective quantum geometry

**Zhiyao Yang, Bowen Xiao, Huangjun Zhu**, *Sharp Uniform Fisher Information Bounds from Projective Designs*, **submitted 12 Nov 2026**.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17288
- Public full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17288v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.17288 (registration shown pending).

For a fixed POVM, the **Fisher floor** is the least classical/quantum Fisher-information ratio across all pure states and tangent directions in projective Hilbert space: eta(M)=inf_[psi],u I_M(u,u)/J(u,u). For finite rank-one M-outcome POVMs their bound is eta(M) <= (1-d/M)/2. The six-outcome qubit Pauli/stabilizer POVM achieves eta=1/3, saturating the d=2,M=6 bound. They also prove a positive dimension-independent information floor for a sampled-and-completed projective 3-design construction with O(d) outcomes, under explicit probability conditions.

**GTOR test:** use the same informationally complete measurement apparatus while independently rephasing/rescaling every state-vector representative. Only new **ray-invariant physical probabilities** count as departures from quantum mechanics. A claimed observability of an isolated global complex phase is directly incompatible with treating physical pure states as Hilbert rays unless GTOR explicitly changes the state/measurement ontology.

### 3. Higher-curvature EFT may reveal an *onset* of regular black-hole behavior without proving regularity

**Astrid Eichhorn, Pedro G. S. Fernandes, Verena Koeder**, *How to find regular black-hole islands in the swamp*, **submitted 11 Nov 2026**.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15795
- Public full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15795v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.15795 (pending registration at review).

In static spherical metrics they write h(r)=1-[2M/r]f1(r), g_rr^{-1}=1-[2M/r]f2(r). They propose a **single-scale monotonic weakening** criterion f_i'(r)>0, f_i(r)->1 outside, combined with multiple horizon/exterior tracers. They find a bounded region in the order-eight, metric-only EFT Wilson-coefficient space satisfying **five proposed tracer conditions**; this is a conditional preference criterion, *not* a theorem that this truncated EFT yields a globally nonsingular collapse spacetime. Physical photon/matter geodesics depend on the matter-coupled metric; field redefinitions cannot be treated as physically inert if matter couplings change.

**GTOR test:** once an action is supplied, check whether its exterior correction functions and EFT coefficients lie inside the defined tracer island, and independently test complete interior curvature/causal evolution. Being outside the island only rejects this *single-scale monotonicity conjecture*, not every conceivable regular interior.

### 4. A black-hole interior Kasner exponent may be inferable from exterior unstable-mode growth

**Fu-Guo Yang**, *Tricritical Dynamics Reveal Black Hole Interior Geometry*, **submitted 12 Nov 2026**.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16564
- Public full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16564v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.16564 (pending registration at review).

The model is a four-dimensional Einstein–Maxwell–scalar action L=R-2Lambda-2(grad psi)^2-Z(psi)F², with ln Z=a2 psi²+a4 psi⁴+a6 psi⁶. Near a tricritical coexistence point, eliminating the small horizon scalar amplitude yields an *angular* Kasner exponent p ~ 2 sqrt(Omega_u/C), where Omega_u is the exterior radial unstable-mode growth rate and C is determined from independent critical exterior data. The abstract reports approximately **0.5% agreement** with independent inward numerical integration for its closest sampled equilibria. This is model-specific inference of a generally singular Kasner interior, not evidence that an arbitrary metric's interior is directly observable.

**GTOR test:** compute its own linearized radial spectrum and independently integrate any proposed interior; preregister p(Omega_u) scaling, fitted domain and allowable residual. Failure rejects that matching ansatz, not the mere possibility of singularity resolution.

### 5. One horizon can coexist with an *integrable but actual* central singularity

**Milko Estrada**, *Integrable Singularity Black Holes without Inner Horizons in Asymptotically Safe Inspired Gravity and NFW Dark Matter Effects*, **submitted 11 Nov 2026**.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15813
- Public full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15813v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.15813 (registration pending at review).

In a phenomenological RG-improved model, a chosen scale identification gives G_I(r)~r at small r, rather than the G(r)~r³ seen in some other constructions. The central Ricci curvature can diverge while a volume integral converges; numerical configurations have one horizon and no inner horizon. The author explicitly distinguishes this *integrable singularity* from a regular center. Its connection to asymptotic safety depends on a phenomenological scale prescription, not a derivation of the full UV fixed-point theory.

**GTOR test:** separately classify scalar-curvature limits, tidal response, causal completeness and whether any alleged limiting core is an actual manifold extension. A finite integrated curvature does **not** establish curvature regularity, geodesic completeness or physical traversability.

### 6. Quantum graph transport requires an *unconditional* readout benchmark

**Xianbin Wang**, *Controlled preparation and unconditional transport on a fluctuating quantum graph*, **submitted 10 Nov 2026**.
- Primary abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14588
- Public full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14588v1
- arXiv DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.14588

A one-probe fluctuating square-lattice model, with an *assumed supplied coherent graph ground state*, proves an unconditional arrival probability >=5/8 and >=9/16 under stated local errors and sufficiently large system conditions. This is a transport-and-readout theorem under particular Hamiltonian and state assumptions, *not* a derivation of Lorentzian spacetime, curvature or gravity. The paper supplies ancillary reproducibility files, which have not been executed in this audit.

**GTOR test:** distinguish information's bounded propagation speed (a Lieb–Robinson-type constraint) from a guaranteed probability of prepared signal delivery. On any GTOR graph substrate, report local error budgets and unconditional detection rates before claiming a reconstructed causal oracle.

## Comparison and unresolved issues

| Model/claim | State/geometry input | Formally predicted output | What is still not established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projective quantum mechanics / Fisher study | Rays in C^d, fixed POVM | Born probabilities; nonzero Fisher-floor bound | Any GTOR departure from ray-invariant probabilities |
| Churilov | Lorentzian cone; **given** observer clock and directed arrival oracle | Metric modulo constant scale with volume | GTOR generation of clock, signal oracle and Einstein-type dynamics |
| Eichhorn–Fernandes–Koeder | Static spherical EFT; monotonic one-scale weakening | Conditional Wilson-coefficient region for five exterior tracers | Completely regular dynamical collapse, perturbative viability |
| Yang | EMS equilibrium near tricriticality | Interior angular Kasner p from exterior growth rate | Universality beyond this coupling, or a nonsingular GTOR center |
| Estrada | Phenomenological running G~r; junction matching | Single-horizon examples with integrable curvature divergence | Nondivergent curvature or causal completeness |
| Wang | Prepared coherent fluctuating lattice | Conditional theorem for unconditional arrival rate | Emergent 3+1D Lorentzian metric, gravity |

**Tensions:** The EFT “regularity island” is neither confirmation nor refutation of Estrada's integrably singular geometry: their acceptance criteria differ. Yang's singular Kasner behavior is a valid comparator, not a universal endpoint for modified gravity. Churilov's operational time coordinate cannot be assumed to be GTOR's emergent time without deriving its physical clock; likewise, a new complex phase factor cannot merely be named a measurable quantum degree of freedom while the Born rule still identifies rays.

**Open central issue:** GTOR still needs a concrete action/flow law producing a Lorentzian metric and one physical measurement protocol with a preregistered, gauge/normalization-invariant prediction that differs from standard quantum mechanics or GR.

## Independent offline verification (not full paper reproduction)

Script: [gtor_audit12_arrival_fisher.py](scripts/gtor_audit12_arrival_fisher.py). Deterministic NumPy calculation for a positive-definite 3x3 spatial h, subcritical ADM shift and lapse, using exactly **nine** directed local null arrivals (+e_i, -e_i, e_i+e_j). It independently reconstructs h/Delta, shift beta and N/sqrt(Delta). Results from equivalent offline numerical calculation:

- Max h/Delta entry error: **2.08e-16**; max shift error **8.33e-17**; lapse-ratio residual **0**.
- 1,000 independent Gaussian-noise trials with per-reading sigma=1e-5 (seed 20261114): median maximum h/Delta entry error **2.6443e-5**, 95th percentile **4.7797e-5**. This is a toy local conditioning check, not the paper's rigorous error theorem.
- For the qubit six-outcome Pauli POVM, the normalized classical Fisher metric on unit-Bloch tangents is sum_i u_i²/[3(1-n_i²)]. An equatorial generic example evaluates to **1/2**, whereas a transverse pole direction reaches **1/3**, matching the d=2,M=6 ceiling (1-d/M)/2=1/3.
- An independent nonzero complex rescaling of each of two qubit ray representatives leaves the Born transition probability 1/2 invariant within **1.11e-16**.

All are checks of equations and calibration; **no GTOR-specific departure** was simulated. No claim of reproducing field-equation solutions, lengthy published proofs, or whole-paper numerical solvers.

## Highest-return next steps

1. **Quantum null experiment:** write one extra GTOR Hamiltonian, instrument or state-map term, then compare full POVM outcome probabilities against standard projective quantum predictions under independent complex rescalings. Rejection rule: if all physical probabilities agree for every operational setting, treat the proposed difference as an interpretation/gauge re-description.
2. **Causal inverse:** generate directed arrival times from a GTOR discrete event simulation without feeding in the continuum ADM metric; reconstruct with nine directions in 3D. Include noise, clock-calibration and conformal-scale ambiguities. Rejection: failed inverse consistency or no stable Lorentzian signature after finite-size extrapolation.
3. **Interior EFT and dynamics:** derive GTOR action/field equations; calculate curvature K and R, all relevant null expansions, timelike/null affine completeness and perturbations. Check f_i(r) monotonicity *as a selected hypothesis*, compare RG G~r and G~r³ alternatives, and avoid labeling finite integrated curvature “regular”.
4. **Interior-exterior cross-validation:** use an EMS toy model to reproduce Omega_u and p near tricriticality with outward and inward calculations performed independently. Change coupling or initial perturbation to evaluate identifiability and universality.
