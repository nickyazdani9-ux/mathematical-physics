## Five new primary papers

1. **Antonino Marcianò, _Correlation Geometry and Emergent Space-Time from Stochastic Correlation Flows_, submitted 10 Nov 2026.**
   - Primary https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14693 ; full public text https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14693v1 ; arXiv DOI https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.14693 (shown pending registration when checked).
   - Physical configurations are measures on self-adjoint operators modulo unitary conjugation. A spectrally gapped, smooth, fixed-rank projector sector supplies local frames and a U(1)/Wilczek–Zee connection, with A=i Ψ†dΨ in the author's convention. Under a deterministic projected gradient ansatz, dP/dτ = -[P,[P,H_eff[P]]], a flow **preserving projector rank**, with τ explicitly an auxiliary parameter, not derived physical time.
   - Its gauge invariant cyclic loop is W_P(C)=Tr[P(x_N)...P(x_1)], approaching an oriented holonomy in a smooth limit. Fast-mode one-loop integration conditionally yields S_eff = ∫√g(Λ_ind + c_R R + [4g_ind²]^{-1}F²+...), but **assumes** a smooth Riemannian continuum metric, a massive stable sector and a Laplace-type operator; does **not derive Lorentzian signature, canonical gravitational constraints or a metric from projectors** (full text §§II,V and Appendix B).
   - GTOR tension: a gauge-invariant projector holonomy is not an independent absolute global wavefunction phase. A mere factor of k in an exact helix connection does not prove new holonomy. An actual closed path/observable and physical coupling are needed.

2. **Yishen Huang, Jiongyue Li, Junbin Li, Xi-Ping Zhu, _The uniqueness of smooth stationary black holes_, submitted 12 Nov 2026.**
   - Primary https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16853 ; public text https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16853v1 ; arXiv DOI https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.16853 (registration pending at inspection).
   - A rigorous proposed proof identifies the **domain of outer communications** of a smooth stationary, asymptotically flat, four-dimensional **vacuum** (Ric(g)=0) rotating single nondegenerate black hole with Kerr **under specified horizon/global conditions**, including bifurcate horizon and maximal hypersurface assumptions. It does not identify the entire interior or apply to modified equations, effective matter, dynamical collapse, degenerate horizons or non-asymptotically-flat solutions.
   - GTOR consequence: a stationary non-Kerr vacuum exterior under all these assumptions must violate at least one premise or the proof. An interior-only GTOR modification can coexist with an ordinary Kerr exterior. Numerical observables must not silently presume exterior departures.

3. **Joydeep Chakravarty, _Imaging the two-sided black hole interior with four-point correlators_, submitted 11 Nov 2026.**
   - Primary https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15933 ; public text https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15933v1 ; arXiv DOI https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.15933 (registration pending at inspection).
   - In an **eternal two-sided AdS/CFT thermofield-double** setting, specially ordered/smeared boundary four-point functions select high-frequency local scattering behind the horizon. Null shocks and shells produce modeled discontinuities. It requires nontrivial ordering, a state-dependent insertion and semiclassical high-frequency/eikonal approximations; finite-N saddle dominance is not established.
   - GTOR relevance: an explicit candidate *theoretical* interior diagnostic beyond shadows/QNMs, but cannot be described as an available telescope observation of astrophysical Schwarzschild interiors. Compare perturbation response after specifying an AdS/CFT dual or leave as a conditional benchmark.

4. **Shinsei Ryu and Yu-ki Suzuki, _De Sitter Gravity and Emergent Time from Fusion Matrix Product States and SYK_, submitted 12 Nov 2026.**
   - Primary https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17045 ; public text https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17045v1 ; arXiv DOI https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.17045 (registration pending at inspection).
   - A sequential spin-fusion quantum channel obeys Σ_α B_α†B_α=I; a final vacuum cap/postselection has success probability Z_N=Σ_{α-vector}|<0|B_{α_N}...B_{α_1}|0>|². Only for Z_N>0 is the capped state normalized by 1/sqrt(Z_N); odd chain lengths have Z_N=0 in the model. In a scaling regime a height random walk selects an invariant time with a Schwarzian action and matches a dS_2 Jackiw–Teitelboim disk amplitude and specified free-field Bunch–Davies two-point functions.
   - GTOR relevance: an unusually explicit example of relational time with **operational normalization/post-selection**, but it is a 1+1-dimensional special duality, **not** a derivation of 3+1D Lorentzian Einstein dynamics or GTOR's proposed black-hole end state. A normalization factor from conditional selection must never be mistaken for an observable free global phase.

5. **Bowen Xiao, Zhiyao Yang, Huangjun Zhu, _Near-Optimal Multiparameter Pure-State Metrology with a Constant Number of Bases_, submitted 12 Nov 2026.**
   - Primary https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17281 ; public text via arXiv; arXiv DOI https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.17281 (registration pending at inspection).
   - Under pure-state projective quantum mechanics and fixed orthonormal-basis measurement families, five independent Haar-random bases give with high probability a positive **dimension-independent** Fisher-information floor; more constant-size families approach the single-copy uniform bound of 1/2. In contrast, even d+1 mutually unbiased bases can have worst-case floor at most 1/(d+1). This complements, without duplicating, 14 Nov's **2611.17288**, on finite projective designs.
   - GTOR test: preregister minimum outcome-probability and Fisher-information deviations over *all* input rays and tangent directions, not only a selected responsive state. A phase effect unaltered by changing physical measurement settings but changed by arbitrary representatives is a representation artefact.

**Material tensions:** (i) an induced Einstein–Hilbert-form action while a Riemannian metric is already supplied does not derive Lorentzian spacetime; (ii) a conventional kinematic geometric phase is not a new observable absolute global phase; (iii) pure-vacuum stationary exterior Kerr rigidity is compatible with alternative interior effective matter, but rejects non-Kerr exteriors satisfying all uniqueness premises; (iv) dS_2 asymptotic time is not a demonstration of de Sitter **black-hole interiors** in 3+1D; (v) holographic interior imaging is not ordinary shadow/ringdown observation.

## Reproducible offline calculation (standard QM control, NOT a GTOR validation)

Code: [scripts/gtor_audit13_projector_holonomy.py](scripts/gtor_audit13_projector_holonomy.py), tested locally using NumPy.

Set a spin-half ray |ψ(φ)>=(cos(θ/2),e^{iφ}sin(θ/2)), θ=π/3, φ_j=2πj/N; P_j=|ψ_j><ψ_j|. The forward Bargmann cyclic product W_N=∏_j<ψ_j|ψ_{j+1}> has phase tending to +2π sin²(θ/2)=π/2, while reversing the loop flips its phase (the reverse-ordered projector matrix product gives the conjugate orientation). Under independent arbitrary site rephasings ψ_j→e^{iχ_j}ψ_j, every P_j and the cyclic product's value remain invariant; the phases telescope. This does not rely on any GTOR-specific Hamiltonian.

- N=16: arg W=1.555509588622, analytic phase error 1.528674e-2.
- N=64: arg W=1.569849520066, error 9.468067e-4.
- N=256: arg W=1.570737184789, error 5.914201e-5; |W|=0.985646489057.
- Independent random local rephasing changes W by ~6.6e-15 at N=256; max ||P²-P|| ~2.4e-16. Reversing ordering gives -1.570737184789. Exact convergence and invariance are *ordinary* projective quantum geometry, not novel GTOR predictions.
- The spinor convention here gives the positive overlap/Bargmann phase; Simon's Berry connection A=i<ψ|dψ> integrates to **minus** this value for the same path; sign depends on transport/ordering convention and must not be silently equated.
