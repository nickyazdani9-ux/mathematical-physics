# Payment and accounting integration contract

This is an engineering specification plus a draft invoice calculator. It does not activate banking, accept money, issue invoices, register an ABN, lodge tax returns or make payments. Private account identities and actual registration status remain in the private accounting and credential systems.

## Separate the authorities

The research worker proposes an expense. The coordinator checks the experiment and budget. Credential Manager authorises a narrowly bounded operation for a specific provider/account/destination/payload/amount/currency/expiry. A payment adapter performs it. The accounting service records the result and reconciles independent provider/bank evidence. The worker receives an opaque result, never the secret.

Incoming revenue, outgoing supplier payments, GPU credits and trading capital are distinct flows. Stripe receipt/settlement does not grant access to arbitrary bank transfers. ANZ PayTo is a merchant agreement authorised through a banking channel; the reviewed page does not establish a universal payment-initiation API for a personal account. Confirm actual account/product eligibility and provider terms before choosing the outgoing adapter. [S24–S26](SOURCES.md)

## Payment intent

Persist an immutable intent with operation ID, entity/account reference, payee and destination fingerprint, reason/project/run ID, quoted maximum, currency, tax treatment status, receipt expectation, approval/grant reference, payload digest and expiry. A standing envelope may authorise routine payments inside verified limits. An LLM cannot mint that envelope or change the destination after authorisation.

Use states DRAFT → AUTHORISED → SUBMITTING → PENDING → SETTLED / FAILED; ambiguous timeouts go to UNKNOWN and reconciliation. Persist the operation before dispatch. External effects are not guaranteed exactly once merely because the local database has a unique key. Prevent duplicate creation, reuse provider idempotency keys where supported, and query/reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying.

Stripe receiving path: hosted Checkout or supported invoicing flow; pinned API version; test mode first; raw-body signature verification using the official SDK; durable event-ID deduplication; asynchronous processing; object reconciliation for unordered events; refund/dispute/fee handling; settlement-to-bank matching. Never trust a browser success redirect as payment proof. API v1 idempotency keys can expire after at least 24 hours, so the local operation ledger must outlive provider deduplication. [S24–S25](SOURCES.md)

Vast credit top-up is an outgoing purchase separate from GPU rental. First integration can record a user-completed top-up and its receipt while the worker uses only approved credit. Automated top-up requires a verified provider payment route and an explicit amount/recurrence ceiling. No automatic debt, overdraft or withdrawal from a personal account follows from a low credit balance.

## Entity and invoice state

The entity profile needs legal/trading name, ABN and verification evidence, GST registration and effective dates, legal form, billing address, bank/provider references and accounting basis. Unknown is distinct from false. Future business-bank migration must preserve old personal-funded expense provenance without relabelling past transactions.

Generate invoices from structured records, with unique numbers, issue date, seller/ABN, item descriptions/quantities/prices, taxable status, GST and totals; buyer identity or ABN is additionally required for tax invoices of AUD 1,000 or more. ATO describes the timing and information requirements, including the general 28-day response to applicable tax-invoice requests. [S21](SOURCES.md#s21)

The prototype uses Decimal arithmetic and item-level rounding, validates ABN format/checksum for tax-invoice drafts, requires known GST status, rejects GST charging when not registered and enforces buyer identification at the threshold. A checksum is not ABN registry verification. Rendered documents remain marked DRAFT and cannot be issued by this module. It handles AUD, positive line items and simple 10% taxable/zero-GST classification only; export supplies, mixed-tax edge cases, credits/refunds and foreign-currency rules need specialist classification.

Store a content hash and revision for every issued document; corrections produce an adjustment/credit record rather than silently overwriting history. A visual stamp is not proof of tax compliance. Supplier expense evidence should be the supplier's original document or a legitimately supported alternative, not a fabricated replacement invoice generated by the agent.

## Expense ledger

Capture receipt/document hash, supplier identity/ABN if applicable, issue/payment/service dates, original currency, conversion rate/source/date, net/tax/gross, payment method and payer, business purpose, project/run allocation, business-use fraction, tax-category rationale, review status and reconciliation links. Personal and business portions stay separate. Do not infer deductibility from the account used, the presence of an ABN or a model's confidence.

ATO requires business purpose and substantiation for deductions, and excludes private/domestic portions. GST credits require registration and other eligibility conditions; a valid tax invoice is normally required for purchases above AUD 82.50. Foreign cloud invoices do not automatically contain Australian GST. Avoid counting a claimable GST credit again as an income-tax deduction. [S22–S23](SOURCES.md)

Ledger examples are provisional accounting mappings: a credit top-up may initially be prepaid services; usage can consume that prepaid balance; provider fees, FX and tax remain separate. Do not expense the top-up and the same usage twice. If an owner pays a business expense personally, record owner funding/reimbursement according to the entity and accountant-approved policy. Final deductibility, R&D treatment, capitalisation and GST classification remain evidence-based review tasks.

Retention policy must be set from the applicable tax/entity rules and preserve records for longer when required. Implement access controls, version history, export, backup and deletion holds. This checkpoint does not claim that a uniform retention period covers every circumstance.

## Integration acceptance

Credential Manager: scoped capability binding, one-dispatch/replay rules, revocation and secret redaction tests. Payments: provider contract, test-mode duplicate/timeout/reordered-event tests, destination binding and reconciliation. Accounting: verified entity profile, invoice numbering, corrected documents, business-use allocation and FX rules. Swarm: cannot bypass payment policy through a browser or worker command. Production tasks belong in the existing private projects; this public repository contains only generic contracts and synthetic fixtures.
